title: About
---

I am writing this as I go.

Not from the other side of anything, not with conclusions I have earned the right to hand out. Just what I notice while I am still in the middle of it: the things I am learning, the things I keep having to learn again, the questions I have not finished answering.

I think most of what shapes a life happens in ordinary moments rather than dramatic ones. What I choose to care about. What I let go. What I am willing to sit with when there is nothing to fix.

So this is a place to think out loud about that, honestly, before it resolves into something tidy.

Nothing here is finished. Neither am I.
